Mr. Weston's Class Rules

Welcome! I am Mr. Weston.
Please do not call me "teacher." I will learn your names soon!

The #1 Rule: The Joy of Quiet

From the moment you walk in until you leave, be quiet.

What does quiet mean?

  • Talk in a whisper
  • Don't talk while I'm talking
  • That's it!

When can you talk louder?

  • While asking a question
  • While answering a question

How to Get My Attention:

  • Raise your hand silently
  • Do not say my name or "Teacher"
  • Wait for me to call on you

What happens if it's too loud?

For the whole class:

  • I will give ONE warning
  • After that, the rest of class will be silent

For YOU:

  • If you cause problems, you may be sent to the tables with NO warning
  • You will sit alone and be silent

How to Enter the Room

  1. Walk in quietly
  2. Sit down
  3. Read the Board for Directions.*
  4. Get Started.

*If nothing is on the board, sit down and wait quietly.

Before the bell rings: If you need water or the bathroom, just go.

After the bell rings: If you are late, you will be marked tardy.

Your Seat

  • You have an assigned seat
  • Sit in the same seat every day
  • Do not ask to move to be by friends
  • I may change seats later

Bathroom Rules

  1. Look at the green/red sign
  2. If it's green, put two fingers up
  3. Wait for me to nod
  4. Get your planner and bring it to me
  5. I will stamp it (keep your planner with you!)
  6. Take the hall pass
  7. Flip the sign to red
  8. When you come back: flip to green, put the pass back, sit down

Food and Drinks

YES: Water with a lid

NO:

  • Food on your desk
  • Snacks from your backpack
  • Soda, cans, or open drinks
  • Anything near the computers

Backpacks

  • Put them under the table OR on your chair
  • Do NOT put them on the table
  • Do NOT leave them in the aisle
  • Do NOT wear them while sitting

Moving Around

You do NOT need to ask to:

  • Throw something away
  • Get a tissue
  • Sharpen a pencil
  • Get hand sanitizer

However:

  • Walk with purpose
  • Do not stop to talk to friends
  • Go and come right back

Headphones

I am waiting for more instructions from administrators. There is currently a no-headphone policy until I receive clarity.

Phones

  • No phones. Ever.
  • Keep phones out of sight and silent
  • If I see or hear your phone, I will put it in an envelope
  • I will take the envelope to the office
  • A parent or other adult on your contact list must pick it up after school

Games and Free Time

Utah HB 273 requires classroom technology to have an educational purpose.

  • You may not play games during free time
  • A game is allowed only when it is part of what we are learning
  • If you finish your work, choose one of the approved class activities

End of Class

5-Minute Bell: Keep working!

1 Minute Left:

  • You can log off
  • You may stand up and stay at your desk
  • Gather your things
  • Stay quiet
  • Clean your station
  • Push in your chair when you leave

Final Bell: Leave quietly

Respect

I expect: Hard work, Focus, Questions, Curiosity

Remember:

  • Sometimes things are hard. That's okay.
  • If something is easy for you, it might be hard for someone else.
  • Never make fun of anyone for not understanding.
  • Struggling means you are learning! Do not be afraid of that feeling.
I am here to help you learn! Let's have a great semester.
